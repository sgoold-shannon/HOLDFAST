# Civilian and squad refinement prompts

Method: built-in image generation. Provisional V2 studies.

## civilians

Create a professional landscape concept-art sheet titled "HOLDFAST / CIVILIAN GROUP STUDIES 01". Serious stylized grounded near-future rural evacuation, same practical subdued military sci-fi art direction as HOLDFAST, no cartoon or glamor. Warm neutral background, three clearly separated equal panels "GROUP A", "GROUP B", "GROUP C". EXACTLY FOUR civilian adults per panel, twelve people total, varied ages including older adults, varied genders and skin tones, ordinary farm-town workwear and everyday clothes. Do not assign gameplay classes or fixed backstories. Each group shows recognizable clothing/value differences and personal belongings: a small soft bag, folded coat, work jacket, modest backpack; never everyone carrying a giant suitcase. Group A earthy rust and cream accents, Group B slate/denim and warm gray, Group C muted sage and tan. Palette differences subtle and supported by silhouettes, not brightly matching uniforms. Main figures in each panel standing close but not overlapping, full body rear three-quarter views appropriate to a high tactical camera. Small bottom inset for each group: compact overhead-ish cluster of the SAME four people, not extra characters. Emphasize readable silhouettes at game scale. No wounds, blood, weapons, panic caricatures, children, military uniforms, neon or hero poses. Bottom caption "FOUR PEOPLE PER COHORT / APPEARANCES PROVISIONAL". Civilians remain dignified and individual. These are concept proposals, not production assets.

## squad

Professional landscape equipment refinement sheet titled "HOLDFAST / SQUAD KIT REFINEMENT 02". Serious stylized 3D concept illustration, grounded near-future US Army field equipment, muted olive/slate, clean neutral background. Four equal columns labelled "MSG / PSYOP", "ORTIZ / MEDIC", "BELL / ENGINEER", "PARK / RECON". Each large full-body REAR three-quarter view with SAME practical modest ballistic helmet shell, role-specific gear. MSG single short flexible radio antenna and narrow tall radio pack; Ortiz adult female medic broad low medical backpack with plain cream identifier NO cross, uniform coverage identical to men; Bell male engineer compact secured tool roll on asymmetric hip harness, no dangling pliers, no loose chain; Park male recon compact slim pack and binocular/notebook pouches. Practical slung rifles muzzle down, fingers clear of triggers. Distinguish silhouettes by equipment not oversized bodies. Hands, hips and shoulders clear for casualty carrying. Bottom small gear callouts: secured radio lead, medical pouch access, retained tool roll, notebook pouch. Add a small simple two-option HEADGEAR inset labelled "TACTICAL HELMET" and "SOFT CAP / NONCOMBAT" to communicate a proposal, not fixed portrait identity. No exact rank insignia, flags or unit patches; leave plain fabric patch areas. Faces provisional and not prominent. No superhero armor, giant pauldrons, bare midriffs or fantasy gadgets. Caption "PROPOSED KIT / VALIDATE AT GAME CAMERA SCALE".
