## Civilian groups

[Design sheet](civilian-groups-01.png) contains three four-person groups plus repeated small group-layout studies. Clothing, ages, appearance and personal belongings are provisional; they do not establish names, relationships, jobs, capabilities or new story facts. This adult-only study is not a campaign-wide restriction on who can appear.

A uses rust/cream, B slate/denim, C sage/tan as restrained material families. Group IDs and order/status indicators remain essential: clothing color alone cannot convey group selection. At night, B may need lighter-value accents to avoid disappearing into ground shadows. Preserve modest personal objects and normal human posture; avoid treating everyone as either an identical crowd or a dramatic panic pose.

Each group remains one logical cohort representing four people. Multiple visual bodies do not imply individual pathfinding, targeting, HP or casualty simulation. The GDD's dispersed/stranded result must not be depicted as four confirmed deaths. Planned visual poses: waiting, following, moving to exit, blocked/waiting for route, and leaving the map. Take-cover commands or protection bonuses are not added by these poses.

In the benchmark, arrange four visual figures inside a readable cohort envelope and test overlap with soldiers and other cohorts, which existing rules allow to share cells. Preserve a clear group marker and pick target when bodies overlap. Do not solve this by adding occupancy restrictions.

## Squad equipment

- MSG: tall narrow radio pack and single flexible antenna; secured communication leads.
- Ortiz: broader lower medical pack; accessible medical pouches and plain identifier area.
- Bell: compact retained asymmetric tool roll, replacing loose/dangling tools.
- Park: slim kit, protected optics and notebook access. The generated pack is still fairly broad: narrow it in the dimensional pass to distinguish him from Ortiz.

Helmets during tactical encounters and soft caps in noncombat scenes are recommendations, not yet owner-approved. These do not add armor statistics. The MSG remains customizable; generated faces are samples. The written brief governs roles; generated annotations such as “obstacle reduction” do not authorize new actions.

Equipment attachment points must leave shoulders, hands, elbows and hips clear for aiming, kneeling, stabilization, pickup and carrying. Verify rifle sling retention and casualty clipping in a simple pose test before finalizing straps and pouches. No rank/patch detail from generated images is authoritative; production insignia should be separately reviewed.
